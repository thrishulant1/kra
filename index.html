About KRA

ನಮ್ಮ ಬಗ್ಗೆ

The Koramangala Realtors Association (R) brings together brokers, builders, promoters and landlords who work in and around Koramangala, Bengaluru. Registered in 2021-22, the association gives its members a common platform to share knowledge, uphold fair practice and speak with one voice on matters that affect the property market in the area.

Beyond business, KRA builds friendship among its members through community and sports events, including the annual KRA Cricket Tournament.

  • Fair practiceHonest, transparent dealings with buyers, sellers and tenants.
  • Member supportGuidance on documentation, regulations and market changes.
  • One voiceRepresenting local realtors before civic bodies and authorities.
  • CommunitySports, celebrations and social service that bring members together.

Real estate in Koramangala

ಕೋರಮಂಗಲದಲ್ಲಿ ರಿಯಲ್ ಎಸ್ಟೇಟ್

Koramangala is a well-planned neighbourhood in south-east Bengaluru, laid out in numbered blocks with wide roads, parks and established residential layouts. It sits close to Hosur Road, the Outer Ring Road and major office hubs, which keeps demand strong for both homes and commercial space.

Property here includes independent houses, apartments, rental homes, shops, offices and restaurant spaces. Because values are high and paperwork matters, buyers, sellers and tenants are best served by local professionals who know each block, its rules and its history.

KRA members can guide you on buying, selling, renting and leasing in any part of Koramangala. Contact the association and we will connect you with a member.

  • Buying or sellingFair pricing, title checks and documentation support.
  • Renting and leasingHomes, shops and offices across all blocks.
  • Local knowledgeMembers who have worked in Koramangala for years.
  • Trusted membersRegistered association with a code of fair practice.
